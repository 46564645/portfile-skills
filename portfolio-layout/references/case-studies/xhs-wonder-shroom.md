## 0. Format and Visual System
- 4 landscape spreads (post shows two per image) + 6 photo images.
- Two alternating grounds: **dark chocolate brown** (cover, making, outcome — night forest, candlelight) and **parchment-beige gradient** (research and design — an old field guide).
- Hand-lettered storybook display face for titles (orange on the cover, moss green for sections); fine serif body; centred small captions.
- Four colours (moss green, burnt orange, ochre, dark brown) map onto the four world regions.
- Photography: dark brown ground, side light, candles, moss and twigs as props.

## 1. Logic Chain
Cover + Inspiration (ethnomycology splits cultures into mushroom-loving and mushroom-fearing → world map) → cultural symbols + folklore reading (West = magic, East Asia = education, Latin America = divinity, Africa = wisdom) → game world-building (4 regions × 4 dimensions) + one design-goal sentence + regional concept paintings + free-roam map sketch → core gameplay loop (sketch, contents, flow) → key components (map, 16 mushroom cards, encyclopedia, quest cards) → making (stone clay) → outcome photos.
One line: **cultural research → narrative matrix → gameplay → components → handcraft → product**. Strong at research → world; breaks at gameplay and validation.

## 3. Block-Writing Patterns
- Inspiration: academic concept → phenomenon in stories → meaning → "I want to design…".
- Folklore cell: region (small) + one keyword (large) + two story examples.
- **World-view matrix**: regions × fixed dimensions (core, role, conflict, traits), 2–4 words per cell, colour-coded through to the product.
- Quest card: Problem → Mushroom → Solution.
- Outcome: noun captions only.

## 4. Why It Works
1. A distinctive research tension (love vs fear) hooks the reader in one paragraph.
2. The 4×4 matrix turns research into level, conflict and item variables.
3. The portfolio itself looks like an adventure field guide (lettering, parchment, candlelight).
4. High-quality handcraft and staged photography.
5. Dark (mood/product) and light (information) spreads alternate.

## 5. Weaknesses
1. No playtest: age range, comprehension of the message, play time all unproven.
2. Rules unclear; quest counts contradict.
3. Research map colours contradict the legend; no sources.
4. Process covers material only — no iteration or rejected options.
5. Inconsistent region names; a leftover placeholder block.

## 6. Completeness
**Incomplete — showcase-heavy.** Present: inspiration/literature, world matrix, concept art, gameplay sketch and flow, components, making, product photos. Missing: target user definition, interviews with children/teachers, competitor games, full rules and turn structure, paper prototype and playtesting, iteration, learning-outcome evidence, sources. Learning value: medium — study the research → matrix → component translation and the unified visual world only.
