## Case-Derived Patterns From Batch 01

## 封面主视觉 + 下方证据分区

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 01, 03, 04, 05, 07, 09, and 10.
- Best for: project opening pages, first research pages, concept introduction pages.
- Structure: a dominant cover/title/hero area sits at the top or first reading zone; supporting research, background, diagrams, or proposal content is organized beneath it in smaller modules.
- Why it helps: the reviewer first understands the project identity, then immediately sees evidence that the project is not only visual atmosphere.
- Works when: the hero image or title area genuinely represents the project's core theme.
- Fails when: the cover image is decorative and the evidence modules below are too small or unrelated.
- Safe adaptation: use the hero area to establish topic and tone, then use the lower modules to prove research, logic, or process.

## 项目色彩身份贯穿全组页面

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 01, 03, 04, 05, 06, 08, 09, and 10.
- Best for: multi-page projects, portfolios with multiple projects, interaction/service/game systems.
- Structure: each project uses a stable color identity across titles, charts, icons, highlights, backgrounds, and interface accents.
- Why it helps: repeated color language makes scattered pages feel like one coherent project system.
- Works when: color is tied to project tone or information hierarchy, not added everywhere equally.
- Fails when: the palette becomes so dominant that text, evidence, or diagrams lose readability.
- Safe adaptation: define a project color role first, such as background tone, highlight, diagram color, or UI accent; do not recolor every element just to look unified.

## 暗色沉浸底 + 高亮信息层

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 01, 06, 08, and 09.
- Best for: speculative design, game design, digital media, HCI installations, future city/system projects.
- Structure: a dark or cinematic base carries atmosphere; bright accent colors, renders, diagrams, labels, or interface elements form the readable information layer.
- Why it helps: the dark base creates strong project mood while highlights guide the eye to important objects or system points.
- Works when: the page still reserves clear text zones, labels, and readable contrast.
- Fails when: atmosphere overwhelms explanation or small text disappears into the background.
- Safe adaptation: use dark atmosphere for identity and scene, but keep key explanation blocks clean and legible.

## 模块化服务/交互拼板

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 03, 04, and 10.
- Best for: service design, UX projects, app/service ecosystems, research-to-solution summary pages.
- Structure: many small components are grouped into visible modules: research, persona, journey, blueprint, concept, UI, storyboard, outcome.
- Why it helps: complex UX/service information becomes scannable because each module has a defined function.
- Works when: each module has a title, visual role, and consistent internal alignment.
- Fails when: modules become equal-sized fragments with no main path or hierarchy.
- Safe adaptation: group related items into larger sections first, then let the viewer move from research to insight to solution.

## 系统地图 + 界面序列并置

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 03, 04, 09, and 10.
- Best for: APP prototypes, platform systems, smart product interaction, service ecosystems.
- Structure: one area shows the system, user flow, service map, or information architecture; another area shows screens or interface states in sequence.
- Why it helps: the reviewer sees both macro logic and concrete interaction output on the same page or spread.
- Works when: the system map and UI sequence clearly refer to each other.
- Fails when: the map is decorative and the UI screens are too small to communicate state changes.
- Safe adaptation: place system logic before or beside the UI sequence, then use labels/lines to show which screen belongs to which step.

## 过程证据矩阵

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, and 10.
- Best for: prototype process, material testing, game development, technical implementation, user testing, iteration pages.
- Structure: process photos, sketches, code/screens, testing shots, material samples, or model iterations are arranged as a matrix or grouped evidence board.
- Why it helps: it proves that the project was developed and tested rather than only rendered at the end.
- Works when: every evidence group has a short label explaining what it proves.
- Fails when: the page becomes a dump of process images without decision logic.
- Safe adaptation: select evidence by function: exploration, test, failure, refinement, final proof.

## 大场景/大渲染 + 技术或交互说明

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 06, 07, 08, and 09.
- Best for: game environments, spatial narrative, future city systems, installations, wearable/technical products.
- Structure: a large render, scene, product photo, or environment image anchors the page; diagrams, detail crops, code/process screenshots, or explanatory notes sit beside or below it.
- Why it helps: the scene creates immediate understanding of the designed world, while the supporting elements prove how it works.
- Works when: the supporting notes explain mechanism, interaction, or use scenario.
- Fails when: the large visual is impressive but the system/interaction logic remains unclear.
- Safe adaptation: pair every large scene with one clear technical, behavioral, or user-experience explanation.

## 叙事世界观展板

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 02, 08, and 09.
- Best for: speculative design, worldbuilding, spatial narrative, digital media, fantasy/future systems.
- Structure: concept text, atmosphere image, object/character/system components, material references, scene renders, and story stages are placed as one visual world.
- Why it helps: it lets the reader enter a fictional or speculative context while still seeing designed components.
- Works when: worldbuilding elements are organized by role or stage.
- Fails when: the page becomes moodboard-only and lacks a design mechanism or user/system relationship.
- Safe adaptation: separate atmosphere, rules, objects, users, and outcomes so the story has structure.

## 游戏机制 + 关卡/场景证据链

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 05, 07, 08, and 10.
- Best for: game design, interactive installation, playable prototype, spatial interaction.
- Structure: introduce game or interaction concept, then show rules/mechanics, user flow, level or scene design, implementation evidence, testing, and final outcome.
- Why it helps: game pages become evaluable as designed systems rather than only attractive screenshots.
- Works when: the chain from concept to playable/interactive evidence is visible.
- Fails when: only final scenes are shown and rules, feedback, or player action are missing.
- Safe adaptation: show what the user does, what changes in the system, and what evidence proves it works.

## 轻底学术网格 + 彩色功能分区

- Evidence scope: observed in Cases 03, 04, 05, 07, and 10.
- Best for: research-heavy pages, service/UX analysis, medical/social topics, clear evidence presentation.
- Structure: a mostly light background supports dense research; color blocks, icons, diagrams, or callout panels divide functions and guide reading.
- Why it helps: the light grid keeps information professional and readable while color prevents the page from becoming flat.
- Works when: color marks hierarchy or category.
- Fails when: too many color blocks compete or the grid becomes visually fragmented.
- Safe adaptation: use color as a navigation layer for sections, not as decoration on every asset.

## Seed Patterns From Existing Tip References

These are allowed because they are already supported by the Tip text, not by unreviewed external case images.

## 三段式排版

- Evidence scope: Tip item 06; visually echoed in Cases 01, 07, and 10.
- Best for: research pages, theory pages, concept introduction pages, pages that need fast logical order.
- Structure: left concept/theory/context, middle visual core or mind map, right support/reference/evidence.
- Why it helps: each area has a clear function, so the reader can understand the page path quickly.
- Works when: the content naturally separates into introduction, core logic, and support.
- Fails when: all three columns are equally dense or equally loud.
- Safe adaptation: keep the functional split, but change imagery, color, and proportions to fit the user's project.

## 灰度素材 + 单色重点

- Evidence scope: Tip items 05, 15, and 18; visually echoed in Cases 02, 05, 06, 09, and 10.
- Best for: social issue pages, research evidence, image-heavy pages, pages with noisy photos.
- Structure: weaken secondary images through grayscale or black/white treatment, then use one accent color to mark key evidence.
- Why it helps: reduces visual noise and makes the important information easier to locate.
- Works when: photos are necessary but currently compete with data or text.
- Fails when: the accent color is applied everywhere or the grayscale treatment hides important detail.
- Safe adaptation: use the accent only for the information the reader must notice first.

## 大图/主视觉 + 局部标注

- Evidence scope: Tip items 14, 16, and 18; visually echoed in Cases 01, 05, 06, 07, 08, and 09.
- Best for: final output pages, prototype proof pages, material/technical detail pages, visual concept pages.
- Structure: one dominant visual carries attention; annotations, circles, lines, or close-ups explain key parts.
- Why it helps: gives the page a visual anchor while preserving explanatory detail.
- Works when: there is a strong image, prototype, interface, scene, or mechanism to anchor the page.
- Fails when: the main visual is decorative and does not communicate project logic.
- Safe adaptation: enlarge the image that proves the core point, not the prettiest image by default.

## 系统图核心 + 周边节点

- Evidence scope: Tip items 08, 10, 12, and 26; visually echoed in Cases 03, 04, 09, and 10.
- Best for: HCI, service design, interaction systems, technical products, ecosystem pages.
- Structure: put the main system/process/model at the center or dominant area, then place users, touchpoints, mechanisms, data, or scenarios around it.
- Why it helps: turns abstract system thinking into visible relationships.
- Works when: the project has multiple actors, stages, touchpoints, or technical modules.
- Fails when: every node has the same weight and no path is readable.
- Safe adaptation: define the central relationship first, then add only necessary supporting nodes.

## 左侧叙事栏 + 右侧成果展示

- Evidence scope: Tip items 13, 19, and 20; visually echoed in Cases 03, 04, 05, 07, and 10.
- Best for: final presentation pages, UI showcase pages, object/product reveal pages.
- Structure: a concise text/navigation column frames what to look for; the larger area shows outcome images, screens, renders, or scenarios.
- Why it helps: separates explanation from visual proof and keeps final pages clear.
- Works when: the final output is visually strong and needs only concise explanation.
- Fails when: the left text column becomes too long or the right-side result lacks detail.
- Safe adaptation: use the text column for orientation, not full project explanation.
